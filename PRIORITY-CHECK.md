# Priority check: the strong product conjecture for Grundy domination

Question: had anyone already disproved Conjecture 22 of Brešar, Bujtás, Gologranc, Klavžar, Košmrlj,
Patkós, Tuza and Vizer, *Dominating sequences in grid-like and toroidal graphs*, Electron. J.
Combin. 23(4) (2016), #P4.34 ([doi:10.37236/6269](https://doi.org/10.37236/6269); arXiv:1607.00248,
where it is Conjecture 6.2), that γ_gr(G ⊠ H) = γ_gr(G) γ_gr(H) for all graphs G and H, or found
the graphs used here?
